Our Story

Why CardioP exists.

Cardiovascular diseases are a growing health challenge across Africa, yet many people lack access to the information, tools, and support needed to understand and manage their heart health.

CardioP was created to bridge this gap. We combine artificial intelligence, preventive healthcare, and community-centered outreach to deliver cardiovascular health solutions that are accessible, understandable, and actionable.

Starting in Nigeria, we are building a platform that puts people first — meeting them where they are, speaking in ways they understand, and supporting them every step of the way toward better heart health.

Africa-First

Built for African communities, by people who understand the challenges.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted digital health companion for cardiovascular wellness across Africa, starting with Nigeria, empowering individuals regardless of socioeconomic status, with the knowledge and tools needed to live longer, healthier lives.

Our Mission

To reduce preventable cardiovascular deaths by providing accessible, evidence-based tools that support early risk identification, medication adherence, and improved health literacy for adults in underserved communities.
